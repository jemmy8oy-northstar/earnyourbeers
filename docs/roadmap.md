# Agile Roadmap: Run for Fun 🏃
*Formerly "Earn Your Beers" - rebranding for App Store compliance*

We follow a staged iterative approach, focusing on the "Logic First" engineering philosophy.

## Stage 1: Absolute MVP 🚀
**Goal**: Log physical activity and rewards, and see a basic balance.
- **Flutter Framework Setup**: Basic project structure and navigation.
- **Manual Logging**: Simple forms for entering runs (KM) and rewards.
- **Core Calculation**: `Net Rewards = (KM / 5) - Spent`.
- **Local Persistence**: Save logs locally.

## Stage 2: The Core Loop 🔄
**Goal**: Weekly cycles and dual-discipline support.
- **Universal Unit**: All logs displayed as Pints.
- **Weekly Settlement**: Automatic Monday reset of the weekly tracker.
- **Running & Cycling**: Refined tracking for the two primary disciplines.
- **History View**: Review past logs and weekly performance.

## Stage 3: Earn Your Vices Rebrand 😈
**Goal**: Pivot to "Earn Your Vices" with customizable guilt-free rewards.
- **Vice System**: Select your vice: Gaming, Netflix, Takeaway, Sweet Threats, Pints, or Wine.
- **Vice Selection**: User picks their vice (Default: Gaming/Non-Alcohol).
- **Measurement**: Generic "Credits" system adaptable to any vice.

## Stage 4: The Vice Vault 🏦
**Goal**: Progression, Strategic Tiering, and Logic.
- **12-Tier XP Journey**: From "Novice" to "Debaucher"? (TBD).
- **Banking (Promotions)**: The 25-point "Savings" requirement to level up.
- **The Vault**: A trophy room for your earned vices.

## Stage 5: Simple Onboarding 📋
**Goal**: Get users into the right league from Day 1.
- **Questionnaire**: Simple 3-question flow to seed initial state.
- **Initial Migration**: Transitioning users from "Base" to their estimated tier.

## Stage 6: Additional Activities 🏊‍♂️
**Goal**: Expand the variety of training.
- **New Tracks**: Support for Walking, Swimming, and Gym sessions.
- **Novelty Bonus**: Multipliers for cross-training.

## Stage 7: Premium Polish ✨
**Goal**: Visual excellence and high-end UX.
- **Vibrant UI/UX**: Glassmorphism, animations, and high-end themes.
- **Promotion/"Bottled It" Scenes**: High-reward feedback loops.
- **Asset Polish**: Custom icons and beer-themed graphics.

## Future Vision: Expansion & Integration 🚀
**Goal**: Move beyond a standalone tool into a connected ecosystem.
- **Wearable Integration**: Sync activities directly from **Strava**, **Garmin**, **Apple Health**, and **Nike Run Club**.
- **Social Cellars**: Share your "Cellar" with friends or join a global leaderboard.
- **Beer Clubs**: Create groups to compete on "Net Savings" over a month.
- **Local Brewery Sync**: Integration with real-world brewery data.
