# Brainstorming: Leagues & Progression (Earn Your Vices)

Now that we have pivoted to **"Earn Your Vices"**, we need a progression system that feels premium, engaging, and neutral (avoiding beer/alcohol branding).

## Theme: "The Path of Discipline"

The progression system should reflect the user's journey of "earning" their rewards through physical discipline.

### The 12 Tiers (Leagues)

Instead of beer-themed tiers (e.g., Lager, Ale, Stout), we move towards titles that reflect **Sophistication** and **Mastery**.

## The XP Logic: "The 25-Point Rule"

Based on the roadmap: *"The 25-point 'Savings' requirement to level up."*

### Concept: "Net Balance Locking"
- **To Rank Up**: User must reach a "Net Savings" of **+25 points**.
- **The "Tax"**: When leveling up, the 25 points are "spent" or "vacated" into a permanent XP pool (The Vault).
- **The Loop**: You can't level up if you are in debt. You must consistently over-perform to progress.

### Banking vs. Spending
- **Vices**: Spent on a day-to-day basis.
- **XP**: Only earned through sustained surplus.
- **Visual**: A "Vault" icon that glows when you have >25 points, inviting you to "Promote" yourself.

---

## Visual Language
- **Colors**: Shift from Gold/Amber (beer) to **Emerald, Sapphire, and Amethyst** (gemstone/premium tiers).
- **Progression Bar**: A sleek, thin line that fills up towards the 25-point mark.
- **The Vault**: A dedicated tab or modal showing your "Total Lifetime Vices Earned" and "Current Rank".
