# Universal Leagues & Progression

Leagues are milestones of fitness and discipline. Progress is calculated during the **Weekly Settlement**.

## Multi-Track Leagues
Every athlete has a level in each major discipline.

| Track | Base Rate (1.0x) | Example Title (Gold) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Running** | 3 km = 1 Pint | Brewmaster Runner |
| **Cycling** | 12 km = 1 Pint | Tour de Beer |
| **Gym/Weights** | 30 mins = 1 Pint | The Stout Lifter |
| **Swim** | 0.4 km = 1 Pint | The Liquid Refiller |

## Difficulty Multipliers
Multipliers apply *only* to the specific track they belong to.

| League Level | Pints to Bottle | Multiplier (Credit Only) | Difficulty Note (Running) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bronze** | 25 Pints | 1.0x | 3km = 1 Pint |
| **Silver** | 25 Pints | 1.6x | 5km = 1 Pint |
| **Gold** | 25 Pints | 2.5x | 7.5km = 1 Pint |
| **Platinum**| 25 Pints | 3.3x | 10km = 1 Pint |

> [!TIP]
> **The Mix-and-Match Strategy**: You can earn pints across any combination of tracks. 
> To promote your **Running** league, it just needs to be your most-used discipline in a successful bottling cycle.
