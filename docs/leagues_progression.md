# Universal Leagues & Progression

Leagues are milestones of fitness and discipline. Updates happen during the **Weekly Settlement**.

## The Placement Week (Future Concept)
*Potential Onboarding Level*: A "Placement Week" to calibrate the user's base fitness. During this week, rates are standard (1.0x) to determine which league they should start in (likely Bronze for most).

## Universal Difficulty Tiers
Leagues are determined by **Lifetime Pints Earned**. 

> [!TIP]
> **Debt Mercy**: If your balance is negative (you are in debt), the multiplier is capped at **1.0x** regardless of your league. This helps you get back to zero faster.
> **Credit Flex**: Once you are in credit, the multiplier kicks in, making it harder to "earn" more beers. This makes a high credit balance a serious flex.

| League | Pints to Bottle | Multiplier (Credit Only) | Difficulty Note (Running) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bronze** | 50 Pints | 1.0x | 3km = 1 Pint |
| **Silver** | 50 Pints | 1.6x | 5km = 1 Pint |
| **Gold** | 50 Pints | 2.5x | 7.5km = 1 Pint |
| **Platinum**| 50 Pints | 3.3x | 10km = 1 Pint |

## Activity Profiles
The app analyzes your last 30 days of data to assign your "Profile Type":
- **Runner**: >70% distance from running.
- **Cyclist**: >70% distance from cycling.
- **Hybrid**: Everything else.

## Why this works:
1. **Prevents Boredom**: As you get fitter, "earning" a beer gets harder, keeping the stakes high.
2. **Short-Term Focus**: The Weekly view prevents you from feeling overwhelmed by total debt or complacent with high credit.
3. **Reward**: Titles and Badges give a sense of prestige.
