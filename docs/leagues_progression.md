# Multi-Track Leagues & Progression

Difficulty follows an exponential curve, but starts with a "Tutorial" phase to accommodate heavy drinkers and beginners.

## The 12-Tier "Master Brewer" Journey
Multipliers apply *only* to the specific track they belong to. 

| Level | League Name | Multiplier | Running (KM/Pint) | Walking (KM/Pint) | Result (1km Run) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Yeast** | 0.2x | 0.20 | 0.40 | **5.0 Pints** |
| **2** | **Hop** | 0.3x | 0.33 | 0.66 | **3.0 Pints** |
| **3** | **Malt** | 0.5x | 0.50 | 1.00 | **2.0 Pints** |
| **4** | **Water** | 0.75x | 0.75 | 1.50 | **1.3 Pints** |
| 5 | **Iron** | 1.0x | 1.00 | 2.00 | 1.0 Pint |
| 6 | **Bronze** | 1.3x | 1.30 | 2.60 | 0.8 Pints |
| 7 | **Silver** | 1.7x | 1.70 | 3.40 | 0.6 Pints |
| 8 | **Gold** | 2.5x | 2.50 | 5.00 | 0.4 Pints |
| 9 | **Platinum** | 4.0x | 4.00 | 8.00 | 0.25 Pints |
| 10 | **Diamond** | 6.5x | 6.50 | 13.00 | 0.15 Pints |
| 11 | **Master** | 10.0x | 10.00 | 20.00 | 0.10 Pints |
| 12 | **Olympian** | 15.0x | 15.00 | 30.00 | 0.07 Pints |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Extreme User**: A heavy drinker (25 pints/week) in the **Yeast League** only needs to run **5km** in a whole week to break even. This allows for a "Path to Redemption" that isn't impossible.
