# Multi-Track Leagues & Progression

Instead of one universal league, users progress through **Discipline Tracks**. This rewards specialists and encourages variety.

## The Track System
Each discipline has its own "Pint Pot" and "League Level."

| Track | Base Rate (1.0x) | Example League Title (Gold) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Running** | 3 km = 1 Pint | Brewmaster Runner |
| **Cycling** | 12 km = 1 Pint | Tour de Beer |
| **Gym/Weights** | 30 mins = 1 Pint | The Stout Lifter |
| **Swim** | 0.4 km = 1 Pint | The Liquid Refiller |

## Track-Specific Scaling
Your difficulty multiplier only applies to that specific activity.

| League Level | Multiplier (Credit Only) | Difficulty Note (Running) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bronze** | 1.0x | 3km = 1 Pint |
| **Silver** | 1.6x | 5km = 1 Pint |
| **Gold** | 2.5x | 7.5km = 1 Pint |
| **Platinum**| 3.3x | 10km = 1 Pint |

> [!TIP]
> **Performance Hack**: If you are a Gold Runner, earning a pint through running is hard (7.5km). But if you are a Bronze Gym-goer, you can "save your week" with a quick 30-min gym session at the easy **1.0x** rate!

## Specialized Titles
Titles are earned based on which activity contributes the most to your balance.

| Profile Type | Runner Title | Cyclist Title | Hybrid Title |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bronze** | Casual Sipper | Weekend Pedaler | Social Mixer |
| **Silver** | Pub Runner | Peloton Pint-Sizer| Multi-Sport Drunker |
| **Gold** | Brewmaster RR | Tour de Beer | Decathlon Drinker |
| **Platinum**| Marathon Brewer | Century Sipper | The Beer Olympian |

## Activity Profiles
The app analyzes your last 30 days of data to assign your "Profile Type":
- **Runner**: >70% distance from running.
- **Cyclist**: >70% distance from cycling.
- **Hybrid**: Everything else.

## Why this works:
1. **Prevents Boredom**: As you get fitter, "earning" a beer gets harder, keeping the stakes high.
2. **Short-Term Focus**: The Weekly view prevents you from feeling overwhelmed by total debt or complacent with high credit.
3. **Reward**: Titles and Badges give a sense of prestige.
