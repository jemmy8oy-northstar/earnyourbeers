# Conversion Rates

The "Earn Your Beers" app uses **1 Pint** as the universal unit of account. All activities earn pints, and all drinks consume pints.

## Drinks (Pint Units)
| Drink Type | Unit | Cost (Pints) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pint of Beer/Cider** | 1 Pint | 1.0 |
| **Bottle of Beer** | 330ml | 0.6 |
| **Glass of Wine** | 175ml | 0.6 |
| **Spirit & Mixer** | Single | 0.4 |
| **Double Spirit & Mixer**| Double | 0.8 |

## The "Specialist" Multiplier
To encourage cross-training and provide a "Week Saver," users get a **Novelty Bonus** for their non-primary activities.

- **Rule**: If an activity accounts for <10% of your last 30 days of volume, it earns at **1.5x** the standard pint rate.
- **Goal**: This represents the "Shock to the System" and allows a specialist (e.g., a pure runner) to "save their week" with a high-value swim or gym session.

## The "Balance" Formula
`Balance (Pints) = Σ(Activities converted to Pints) - Σ(Drinks consumed)`
