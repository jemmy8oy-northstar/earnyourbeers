# The Brewery Bank & Bottling

To make progress permanent and comparable across leagues, we've introduced the **Banking & Bottling** mechanic.

## 1. Bottling (Promotion)
Credits aren't just numbers; they are assets. Once you reach a **Promotion Threshold**, you "Bottle" your pints.

| League | Promotion Threshold | Action | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bronze** | 20 Pints | **Bottle & Promote** | 20 Pints moved to Vault. Start Silver at 0. |
| **Silver** | 50 Pints | **Bottle & Promote** | 50 Pints moved to Vault. Start Gold at 0. |
| **Gold** | 100 Pints | **Bottle & Promote**| 100 Pints moved to Vault. Start Platinum at 0. |

## 2. The Vault (Accumulated Wealth)
Pints in your **Vault** are permanent. They represent your "Net Worth" in the app.
- **Vaulted Pints** never expire.
- They are used to calculate your **Total Pint Score**.

## 3. The Bailout (Debt Protection)
If you go into **Deep Debt** (e.g. -5 pints), you can "Break a Bottle" from your Vault to reset your current balance to zero.
- **Cost**: You lose the vaulted pints.
- **Risk**: If you lose too many vaulted pints, you might be **Demoted** back to the previous league.

## 4. Comparability: The "Pint Score"
How do you compare a Bronze runner to a Platinum runner? Using the **Pint Score**.

`Pint Score = Σ (Vaulted Pints * League Multiplier) + (Current Credit * League Multiplier)`

### Example Scenarios:
- **User A (Bronze)**: 50 Vaulted Pints. `50 * 1.0 = 50 Score`.
- **User B (Gold)**: 10 Vaulted Pints. `10 * 2.5 = 25 Score`.
- **User C (Silver)**: 25 Vaulted Pints. `25 * 1.6 = 40 Score`.

**Result**: User A is leading the leaderboard because they have been consistently "earning" more at a lower intensity, but User B is gaining fast because their multiplier is 2.5x.

## Why this is fun:
1. **Strategic Decisions**: "Should I promote to Silver now, or stay in Bronze to farm some easy points for the leaderboard?"
2. **Safety Net**: Heavy drinking weekends don't end your journey; they just "cost" your banked progress.
3. **True Competition**: It’s not just about how fast you run, but how much you "save" over time.
