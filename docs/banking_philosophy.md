# The Brewery Bank & Bottling

The banking system handles how your short-term hard work becomes long-term prestige.

## 1. Bottling & Promotion (Primary Discipline)
Promotion is a strategic choice made at the **Weekly Settlement**.

- **The Threshold**: Once your **Net Savings** (Pints Earned - Pints Drunk) across all tracks reach **25 Pints**, you can "Bottle & Promote."
- **The Philosophy**: This 25-pint target is a "Savings Goal."
    - A **Light Drinker** (2 pints/week) can promote in 2-3 weeks of moderate running.
    - A **Heavy Drinker** (20 pints/week) must exercise significantly more to "save" those 25 pints, reflecting the higher burden of their lifestyle.
- **Primary Promotion**: The track that contributed the **most pints** (gross) to the 25-pint savings is the one that promotes. 
- **The Boost**: This rewards multi-discipline athletes by allowing them to progress using any activity, while ensuring their "strongest" activity gets harder to prevent it from carrying them forever.

## 2. The Vault & The Debt Floor
When you promote with **X Pints**:
- **Vault**: Those X pints are moved to your **Lifetime Vault** as a specific bottle (e.g., a "Silver Running Bottle").
- **Debt Floor**: Your current balance resets to 0.0, and you gain a **Debt Floor of -X**. 

## 3. "Bottled It" (Automatic Demotion)
> [!NOTE]
> **Sensitivity Check**: Is "Bottled It" too insensitive? While it fits the brewery theme of the app perfectly, we must ensure it doesn't cross the line from "fun rivalry" to "discouragement." We will monitor this during testing.

If your weekly settlement brings your total balance below your **Debt Floor**:
1. **Automatic Demotion**: You are demoted in your highest-league track.
2. **Bottle Loss**: One bottle (**25 pints**) is removed from your Vault to pay the debt.
3. **Reset**: You start at 0.0 in the lower league.

> [!WARNING]
> **"Bottled It"** is the safety valve. It resets the difficulty so you can find your feet again, but at the cost of your "banked" progress.
